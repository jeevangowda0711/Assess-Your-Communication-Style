# Assess Your Communication Style

## 📌 Overview
This repository contains my completed lab for **"Assess Your Communication Style"**, a hands-on exercise aimed at helping Product Managers identify and understand their dominant communication style. The lab involved completing a structured Excel-based survey across four communication dimensions—Analytical, Structural, Conceptual, and Social.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify your personal communication style using a structured self-assessment.
- Understand the characteristics of Analytical, Structural, Conceptual, and Social styles.
- Reflect on how communication preferences impact stakeholder engagement and collaboration.
- Learn tips for effectively communicating with different styles.

## 📝 Lab Summary
In this lab, I:
1. Completed a detailed communication survey spread across four worksheets (Analytical, Structural, Conceptual, Social).
2. Scored each dimension on a 1–5 scale based on how well each statement matched my personal preferences.
3. Calculated the total scores and reviewed the dominant communication style.
4. Reviewed best practices for engaging with each communication style in team environments.

## 📊 My Communication Style Results
| Communication Style | Score   |
|---------------------|---------|

| **Conceptual** (Dominant) | **29.73** |

**Dominant Style:** 💡 **Conceptual** – I value creativity, big-picture thinking, and brainstorming. I thrive in environments that allow for innovation, abstract thinking, and flexible planning.

## 🧠 Style Insights & Communication Tips

- **Conceptual Communicators:**
  - Thrive on vision, possibilities, and innovation.
  - Prefer open-ended discussions and visual thinking.
  - May overlook details unless absolutely necessary.
  - Respond well to metaphors, future-oriented thinking, and collaborative ideation.

- **How to Communicate with Me:**
  - Start with the “why” and vision.
  - Allow time for brainstorming.
  - Use diagrams or big-picture visual aids.
  - Encourage creativity and idea exploration.

## 🚀 Key Takeaways
- Communication is not one-size-fits-all. Understanding your style improves team collaboration.
- Conceptual thinkers are future-oriented and drive innovation—but must remain grounded in execution details.
- Knowing your style empowers you to adapt when communicating with stakeholders who may have different preferences.
